# How to Cook the Perfect Laksa –

## Ingredients

- 8 large raw, shell-on prawns
- 4 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 litre water
- 2 lemongrass stalks, lightly crushed
- 50g laksa leaves (hot mint), or a mix of coriander and mint, plus extra to serve
- 400ml coconut milk
- Sugar, salt and pepper, to taste
- 8 cubes deep-fried tofu (shop-bought is fine)
- ¼ cucumber, deseeded and finely shredded
- 200g wide flat dried rice noodles or vermicelli
- For the paste:
- 10 dried chillies, soaked
- 30g dried shrimp, soaked
- 75g ginger, peeled
- 6 garlic cloves, peeled
- 4 small Asian shallots (about 50g), peeled
- 30g shrimp paste
- 2 tbsp ground coriander
- 1 tbsp ground turmeric
- 5 macadamia nuts
- 100g pork mince
- 2 tbsp cornflour
- 1 tsp fish sauce
- ¼ tsp white pepper
- To serve:
- 4 red bird’s eye chillies, chopped
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Directions

1. Soak the chillies and dried shrimp in hot water for 30 minutes, then drain. Put in a small food processor with all the other paste ingredients and whizz fairly smooth.
2. Peel the prawns, then fry the shells and heads in oil.
3. Shell the prawns and set the meat to one side. Heat the oil in a large saucepan, then add the paste and prawn shells, and fry, stirring, until dark and aromatic – about 10-15 minutes. Beat in the water, then add the lemongrass and laksa leaves, and bring to a boil. Turn down the heat and leave the broth to simmer gently for 30 minutes.
4. Add water, lemongrass and laksa leaves, and cook for 30 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, if you’re making the prawn balls, put the prawn meat in a food processor with all the remaining prawn ball ingredients and whizz to a chunky paste (otherwise, keep the prawns whole and add to the broth for the last minute of cooking). Form the prawn mixture into eight balls; wet your hands first, to make it easier to handle.
6. Blitz the prawn meat with pork mince, cornflour, fish sauce and pepper, then form into balls.
7. Pass the prawn stock through a fine sieve to remove the solids, squeezing out as much liquid as possible, then return the broth to the pan. Whisk in the coconut milk, return to a boil, then stir in the tofu puffs and prawn balls, and simmer gently for 15 minutes.
8. Strain the prawn stock through a sieve, then stir in the coconut milk. Once simmering, poach the prawn balls and tofu.
9. Meanwhile, cook the noodles according to the packet instructions, then divide between four bowls.
10. Pound the fresh bird’s eye chillies with a little coarse salt to make a coarse paste. Check the broth for seasoning, then pour it all over the noodles. Top with the tofu and prawn balls (or whole prawns), shredded cucumber and a little fresh coriander.
11. Ladle the broth over cooked rice noodles, top with the tofu, prawn balls and herbs, and serve with lime and chilli.
12. Serve with the fresh chilli paste and lime wedges on the side.
