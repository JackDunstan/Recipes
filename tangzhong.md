# Tangzhong

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp (18g) unbleached all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp (30ml) filtered water
- ¼ cup (60ml) whole milk

## Directions

1. Make the tangzhong. In a small saucepan, whisk together the all-purpose flour, water, and milk until completely dissolved. Heat over medium heat, stirring continuously, until the mixture thickens into a thick paste. Transfer to a small bowl, and set aside to cool.
